# Transaction Review Thresholds (SAMPLE POLICY - written for this demo)

Transactions with a model fraud probability above the alert threshold must be routed to manual review within 15 minutes. High-value transactions above the escalation amount require a second reviewer regardless of the score.

Transactions scoring just below the threshold are monitored and may be sampled for quality assurance. Reviewers should record the reason for every release or block decision.

A transaction that is both high amount and unusual relative to the cardholder's normal spending pattern is treated as high risk and should be held pending customer contact.
