# Customer Contact and Holds (SAMPLE POLICY - written for this demo)

When a transaction is held, the customer should be contacted through their registered channel and asked to confirm the transaction. If the customer cannot be reached, the hold stays in place and the case is escalated after 24 hours.

False alerts create friction. Reviewers should prefer a quick confirmation message over a card block when the risk score is moderate and no other warning signs are present.

Confirmed fraud cases lead to card replacement, a chargeback process and a feedback label for model retraining.
